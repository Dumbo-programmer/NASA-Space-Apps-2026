# Ignis_mu_NASA_flammability_dataset_v0.1.xlsx
**Source:** Hirsch et al. 2011, Selected Parametric Effects on Materials Flammability Limits (NTRS 20110008636), Tables 2-9. 52 rows.
**Inputs:** material, form, thickness, mounting, ignition method, burn-length criterion, test pressure.
**Outputs (observed):** ULOI (% O2), MOC (% O2).
**Derived:** MOC band at test pressure; pressure-adjusted MOC estimate at 8.2 psia (calculated, needs Physics Lead approval).
**Use:** 1g certification baseline to compare with microgravity results (e.g. BASS-II PMMA).
**Status:** verification pending (yellow columns).
